## The problem

In Pakistan, finding somewhere to eat mostly means Facebook food groups, Instagram reels shaped by free tastings and paid promotion, and Google Maps listings with thin ratings. Diners trust the hype and get a bad meal. Byters gives them structured reviews from real diners instead.

## The hard call

Once a restaurant has at least five reviews, Byters writes an AI summary of what people recommend and what they say to avoid. Feeding the model the latest reviews would mostly repeat the loudest mood, so it gets a fixed mix: 10 recent positive reviews (4 stars and up), 5 mixed and 5 negative. That keeps a well-loved place's real weak spots in the summary.

## What I built

We were a team of three and I led it. Reviews are rated on taste, service, ambience and value, with dish tags and photos. Search understands queries like "crispy burger in gulberg". I also built Where to Eat with a teammate: restaurant suggestions for one person or a group, using vector embeddings.
