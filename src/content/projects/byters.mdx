## Context

Byters is a food review platform. People rate restaurants, write reviews and keep a profile of their own, and the platform helps them discover where to eat next.

## What I built

- A full-stack review platform where users rate restaurants, write reviews and manage dynamic profiles.
- Secure authentication for user accounts.
- AI-powered restaurant discovery using vector embeddings.
- A group decision feature to facilitate collaborative choices.

## Stack notes

Built with Next.js and MongoDB, with Redux and Tailwind CSS on the front end, and deployed on Vercel.
